Textile Science & Engineering

Academic Research

Undergraduate research at the intersection of sustainability and textile chemistry.

Final-Year Research Project · 2024

Reuse of Spent Dye Bath in Cotton & Viscose Dyeing

Institution BUTEX (Wet Process)
01

Objective

Investigate whether spent dye baths in reactive dyeing of cotton and viscose can be reconstituted and reused without compromising shade depth, wash-fastness, and rubbing fastness.

02

Method

Collected spent dye baths after exhaustion, measured residual salt and dye spectrophotometrically, reconstituted electrolyte and alkali concentration, and performed multi-cycle re-dyeing.

03

Outcome

Successful reuse across 2–3 cycles with color difference ΔE < 1.0 (commercial tolerance), achieving a 42% reduction in water consumption and 38% decrease in effluent salt load.

Sustainability Impact: High water conservation potential for industrial dye houses in Bangladesh.
ΔE < 1.0 Commercial Grade
